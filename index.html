CLEAR ANSWERS

Good to know.

Which businesses can use Pleniora?

Retail, clothing and fabric shops, restaurants, pharmacies, grocery stores, electronics, salons, bakeries, wholesale, hardware, auto parts and other general businesses can sign up.

Will it guide me when I log in?

Yes. A first-login tour introduces the main areas and adapts the setup tip to your chosen business. Use “How to use” inside the app for a guide to your current module.

Is FBR or PRA connected right now?

No live tax authority connection is enabled in this build. Digital invoicing integration requires the appropriate authority process and an eligible integrator. Confirm your business obligations and setup route with FBR/PRA before relying on tax invoices.

Are online subscription payments active?

Not yet. The plan prices are shown for reference; a payment provider and verified billing flow must be configured before online payments are accepted.
