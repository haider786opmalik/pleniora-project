Pharmacy POS questions

Can I record medicine batch and expiry details?

Product records include batch or lot number and expiry date fields. The current edition does not provide regulated dispensing, prescription checks or automated expiry compliance.

What are the plan prices?

The trial is free for 30 days. The monthly plan is Rs 2,000 and the yearly plan is Rs 20,000. Subscription payment checkout needs a configured payment provider.
