Restaurant POS questions

Can I manage dine-in tables?

Yes. Add tables, open a ticket, follow it in the kitchen queue and use the POS to bill it.

Can I use it for takeaway orders?

Yes. Start a takeaway or delivery ticket from Tables & Orders and follow it through to payment.

How much does Pleniora cost?

The trial is free for 30 days. The monthly plan is Rs 2,000 and the yearly plan is Rs 20,000. Online subscription checkout still requires a configured payment provider.
